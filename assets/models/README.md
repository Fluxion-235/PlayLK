# PlayLK 3D Model Assets Directory

Place your custom `.glb` or `.gltf` 3D models in this folder (`assets/models/`).
`app.js` is configured with `ASSET_PATHS` to load from these paths when switching from the procedural low-poly Three.js models to external `.glb` meshes:

## Primary Sport Models (Replaces Selection Buttons)
- `assets/models/football.glb` — Football / Futsal ball
- `assets/models/pickleball.glb` — Perforated pickleball
- `assets/models/tennis_ball.glb` — Tennis ball
- `assets/models/shuttlecock.glb` — Badminton shuttlecock

## Secondary Hover & Strike Action Models (Appears on Hover, Swings/Kicks on Click)
- `assets/models/football_leg.glb` — Player leg & football cleat (kicks ball on click)
- `assets/models/pickleball_paddle.glb` — Pickleball paddle (hits pickleball on click)
- `assets/models/tennis_racket.glb` — Tennis racket (drives tennis ball on click)
- `assets/models/badminton_racket.glb` — Badminton racket (smashes shuttlecock on click)

## 3D Court Scene Models (Shown After Page Transition)
- `assets/models/football_pitch.glb` — 3D Football / Futsal pitch
- `assets/models/pickleball_court.glb` — 3D Pickleball court
- `assets/models/tennis_court.glb` — 3D Tennis court
- `assets/models/badminton_court.glb` — 3D Badminton court
